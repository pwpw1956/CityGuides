What the adaptive guide does

You do not have to start at Stop 1. “I AM HERE” records your starting point and finds the best nearby way into the tour. The guide has more sights than a normal 2–2½ hour tour needs, so it can choose a shorter or longer selection according to your time and today's availability.

While you walk, the app measures elapsed time and your actual progress. If the remaining plan no longer fits, it can propose dropping or postponing lower-priority stops. It will ask before changing the plan.

Home / finish

Your “Home” is the place where you pressed “I AM HERE”. Once the tour has started, the button below appears as a guarded long-press control.

Data and connectivity

The core tour data is stored with the app. Opening rules are calculated locally; live overrides are refreshed from the guide's city-specific live status file. Walking routes are calculated online when possible and the last successful route remains visible if routing becomes unavailable.
