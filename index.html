How CityGuides works

  1. Choose a city and how much time you have.
  2. Go to the place where you want to start and press I AM HERE. That becomes Home.
  3. The guide chooses a suitable set of sights from a larger repertoire. You can start anywhere along the route.
  4. Use Map to see the pedestrian route and coloured site markers. Use Detail for the current place and its audio description.
  5. With Auto-follow ON, GPS automatically changes the guide section when you enter an appropriate site zone. Turn it off if you prefer to control sections yourself.
  6. The plan can be re-evaluated as you walk. If you fall behind, the guide can propose dropping lower-priority stops and asks before changing the plan.
  7. GET ME HOME is deliberately disabled until a tour has started. Hold it briefly, then confirm, to end the sightseeing tour and route you back to Home.

Why some places are not selected

Orange means the place is available but was left out because the time, walking involved or its priority made another combination better. Green means it is a useful optional stop if time permits. Red means it is closed or otherwise unavailable. Blue means it is on today's current plan.
