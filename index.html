La Rochelle — self-guided walking tour

About 125–145 minutes • about 3 km • designed for a mostly level walk

GPS not started Tap “Use my location” when you are ready.

The audio uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech voice. No account or paid service is required.

How the automatic mode works

The guide uses zones, not a single exact GPS point. Large public spaces have a larger zone; individual buildings have a smaller one. When GPS puts you inside another zone, that section is selected automatically. You can switch automatic selection off and use Next/Previous instead.

GPS accuracy can vary, especially beside tall buildings. The app therefore uses practical zone radii rather than expecting a five-metre fix.

Accessibility

The main route is deliberately level and avoids making the interiors of the medieval towers part of the timed tour. The Tour de la Lanterne and other tower interiors are optional because they involve stairs.

For the developer / future cities

This prototype is data-driven: the tour content, coordinates, zones, images and route are all in tour.json. To make another city, replace that file rather than rewriting the application.
