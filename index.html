Map pin colours

🔵 Blue — selected stop
🟠 Orange — planner alternative
🟢 Green — rejected by you
🔴 Red — unavailable
⚫ Grey — visited

Changing the route

Only blue places are numbered and counted as stops. ADD promotes orange to blue. Removing a blue stop makes it green. RESTORE changes green back to orange. Press Re-plan after making your changes.

Tour progress

Completed stops become grey. The progress bar and Stop X of Y show progress through the approved tour.
